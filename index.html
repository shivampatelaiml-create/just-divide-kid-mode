How To Play

Drag the top tile of the stack into an empty square. If it touches an equal tile, both disappear. If the bigger number divides evenly by the smaller one, they merge into the answer (12 next to 3 becomes 4). Use KEEP to save a tile for later, or TRASH to throw one away.
